Essential GD Do's & Don'ts
DO's:
  • Maintain eye contact with the entire group, not just the evaluator.
  • Use the PREP structure (Point, Reason, Example, Point).
  • Listen actively and build constructively on others' arguments.
  • Keep a composed, professional tone of voice.
DON'Ts:
  • Do not interrupt others aggressively or raise your voice.
  • Do not speak without clear data or relevant context.
  • Do not dominate the discussion continuous speaking time.
  • Do not engage in personal side arguments.
